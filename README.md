## Course topics

1. AI Ethics Foundations
2. Responsible AI Principles
3. AI Governance Frameworks
4. AI Governance Lifecycle
5. Fairness and Explainability
6. Data Governance and Privacy
7. Generative AI Security and Copyright
8. Human Oversight and Accountability

The course covers the Singapore Model AI Governance Framework, AI Verify, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, data stewardship, privacy-enhancing techniques, fairness metrics, explainability, OWASP LLM risks, layered generative AI controls, copyright and provenance, human-in/on/out-of-the-loop models, escalation, redress and accountable deployment decisions.

# Activity 8 — Design Human Oversight and a Governance Decision

**Course Proposal alignment:** LU2 · LO2 · A2, A3

**Goal:** Select meaningful human involvement, escalation, monitoring and redress for an AI deployment (A2, A3).

**Deliverable:** Oversight matrix and governance board decision pack

**Duration:** 30 minutes

## Files

- `Prompt Pack.pdf` — approved prompts and safe-use reminders.
- `checklist.md` — submission and acceptance checklist.
- `decision-scenarios.csv` — supplied mock input or working template.
- `governance-board-decision.md` — supplied mock input or working template.

## Step-by-step

1. Open decision-scenarios.csv and score severity, reversibility, uncertainty, scale and vulnerability.
2. Choose an oversight model and explain why weaker or stronger options are unsuitable.
3. Define information shown to the reviewer, authority to override/pause and maximum response time.
4. Add escalation triggers for low confidence, vulnerable people, complaints, drift and control failure.
5. Design notice, factual correction, independent appeal and remedy pathways.
6. Prepare and sign a board decision with conditions, monitoring thresholds, incident triggers and review date.

## Acceptance check

The oversight design is risk-proportionate, gives reviewers real authority, addresses automation bias and includes contestability and stop conditions.

## Safety and privacy

Use only supplied mock data. Do not enter personal, confidential, assessment or client information into a public AI system.

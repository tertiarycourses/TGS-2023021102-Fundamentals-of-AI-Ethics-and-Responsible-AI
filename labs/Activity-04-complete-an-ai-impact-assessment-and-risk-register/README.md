# Activity 4 — Complete an AI Impact Assessment and Risk Register

**Course Proposal alignment:** LU1/LU2 bridge · LO1/LO2 · K3, A1, A3

**Goal:** Apply the governance lifecycle to assess impact, select controls and make a recorded go/no-go decision (A1, A3).

**Deliverable:** AI impact assessment, risk register and stage-gate decision

**Duration:** 50 minutes

## Files

- `Prompt Pack.pdf` — approved prompts and safe-use reminders.
- `checklist.md` — submission and acceptance checklist.
- `deployment-change.md` — supplied mock input or working template.
- `impact-assessment.md` — supplied mock input or working template.
- `risk-register.csv` — supplied mock input or working template.
- `stage-gate-decision.md` — supplied mock input or working template.

## Step-by-step

1. Read deployment-change.md and record purpose, scope, scale, users, affected people and alternatives.
2. Write at least six cause-event-impact statements in risk-register.csv.
3. Score likelihood and severity before controls and identify the risk owner.
4. Add preventive, detective, responsive and recovery controls with evidence and test frequency.
5. Re-score residual risk and identify conditions, exceptions and a review date.
6. Write a go, conditional-go or no-go decision signed by the accountable owner.

## Acceptance check

The assessment connects context, harms, controls, evidence, residual risk and an accountable decision with conditions.

## Safety and privacy

Use only supplied mock data. Do not enter personal, confidential, assessment or client information into a public AI system.

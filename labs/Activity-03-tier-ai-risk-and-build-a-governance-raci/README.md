# Activity 3 — Tier AI Risk and Build a Governance RACI

**Course Proposal alignment:** LU1/LU2 bridge · LO1/LO2 · K1, K2, K3, A1, A3

**Goal:** Apply governance frameworks to classify AI use cases and assign decision rights (A1, A3).

**Deliverable:** AI inventory, risk-tier rationale and governance RACI

**Duration:** 60 minutes

## Files

- `Prompt Pack.pdf` — approved prompts and safe-use reminders.
- `checklist.md` — submission and acceptance checklist.
- `ai-inventory.csv` — supplied mock input or working template.
- `raci.csv` — supplied mock input or working template.

## Step-by-step

1. Open ai-inventory.csv and confirm each row has an owner, purpose, data, model/provider and deployment state.
2. Score severity, scale, reversibility, affected-person vulnerability and autonomy from 1 to 4.
3. Calculate inherent risk and assign low, medium, high or prohibited with a written rationale.
4. Map the Singapore MGF, NIST AI RMF and ISO 42001 practices to one shared control set.
5. Complete raci.csv for seven lifecycle decisions and check for one Accountable owner per row.
6. Define approval thresholds and review frequency for each tier.

## Acceptance check

All use cases have a defensible tier, minimum control profile, review cadence and unambiguous RACI.

## Safety and privacy

Use only supplied mock data. Do not enter personal, confidential, assessment or client information into a public AI system.
